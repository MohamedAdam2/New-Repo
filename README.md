## Project Overview
This project analyzes air quality data collected across Nebraska between February 2024 and March 2025. The goal is to evaluate air quality conditions and determine whether pollutant levels meet the National Ambient Air Quality Standards (NAAQS). The analysis focuses on identifying trends, extreme values, and potential public health concerns.

## Objectives
- Identify locations with the highest mean and median concentrations of VOC, PM2.5, and PM10  
- Determine where maximum pollutant values occurred  
- Analyze how environmental factors such as temperature and humidity relate to air quality  
- Evaluate whether any locations experienced unhealthy AQI levels  
- Provide research-based explanations for trends and outliers  

## Data and Methods
Air quality data were obtained from AirPurple and processed using Python in a Jupyter Notebook. The data were cleaned, organized, and analyzed to calculate summary statistics by sensor location. Environmental variables and EPA AQI guidelines were used to support interpretation of the results.
